Numerology Glossary & Meanings

A comprehensive guide to understanding your core chart numbers, cycles, pinnacles, and hidden traits.

Your Core Numbers

☼

Life Path Number

Calculated from your complete date of birth, this is the most critical number in your chart. It reveals your overarching life purpose, core lessons, natural tendencies, and the path you are meant to walk during this lifetime.

Birthdate Number

Derived strictly from the specific day of the month you were born. It highlights specific talents, unique traits, and short-term abilities that support your main Life Path journey.

Attitude Number

Calculated using your birth month and birth day, this number dictates your default outer demeanor, determining how you approach new situations and the initial impression you project to the world.

Expression / Essence Number

Derived from all the letters of your full birth name, this reflects your natural abilities, potential talents, and the physical/mental capacities you express while pursuing your goals.

Soul Urge Number

Calculated from the vowels in your full birth name, this reveals your inner heart's desire—your deep emotional motivations, secret cravings, and what truly makes you feel fulfilled inside.

☼

Maturity Number

A combination of your Life Path and Expression numbers, this indicates the underlying direction, ultimate wisdom, and personal realizations that unfold as you grow older (typically manifesting in the second half of life).

High Life Path Number

Your unreduced Life Path number that reveals more insight on your reveals your overarching life purpose.

Personal Cycles & Timeframes

☼

Personal Year

A yearly forecast calculated by combining your birth month, birth day, and the current calendar year. It defines the specific thematic focus, lessons, and opportunities governing your life from one birthday to the next.

Personal Month

Derived by adding the current calendar month's digit to your Personal Year value. It breaks down monthly pacing, shifting energies, and short-term adjustments.

Personal Day

Calculated by adding a specific calendar day's digits to your Personal Month number, giving you granular daily insight into optimal timing for tasks and interactions.

Hidden Traits & Karmic Influences

The Hidden Passion Number(s)

Identified by looking at which numbers repeat most frequently in your full name. It highlights your dominant personality strengths, intense inclinations, and specific talents you love exercising.

The Karmic Lesson Number(s)

Exposed by missing numbers (gaps) in your name's letter breakdown. These point out weak spots, character traits you need to develop, and life lessons you are here to master.

The Subconscious Self

Calculated based on how many broad categories of numbers are present in your name. It details how you react under crisis, pressure, or unexpected emergencies.

Life Epochs: Cycles, Pinnacles & Challenges

☼

The First, Second, and Third Period Cycles

The three major long-term developmental chapters of your entire life (youth, maturity, and wisdom years), each governed by a specific numerical vibration derived from your birth month, day, and year.

The First, Second, Third, and Fourth Pinnacle Cycles

Four major achievement peaks or developmental phases spanning your life. They provide insight into external opportunities, optimal environments, and achievements to target during each specific age bracket.

The First, Second, Third (Main), and Fourth Challenges

The underlying psychological hurdles and obstacles associated with your life stages. Identifying your challenges helps you navigate friction points, break negative behavior loops, and foster emotional maturity.
